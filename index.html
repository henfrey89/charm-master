Guide

How to Play

Everything you need to know about earning charms, trading spares and climbing the ranks.

How it works The eight charms Ranks Rules
  1. 1Earn. When someone does a task well, a teammate grants them a charm and says why.
  2. 2Reveal. The charm is drawn at random. All eight are equally likely and worth exactly the same.
  3. 3Collect. Charms go on your bracelet. A second copy of a charm is a spare.
  4. 4Trade. Post spares on the Open Market and take deals that give you the charms you are missing.
  5. 5Rank up. Complete a full set of eight to rise a rank. Three sets in one month makes you a Charm Master, and each rank lets you grant more charms a day.

Every charm is equally likely and worth exactly the same. Collect one of each to complete a set.

Every full set of all eight charms ranks you up and uses up one of each charm, so you can keep collecting. The ladder is sized so the top rank can be reached within one month, and ranks start again each round. Higher ranks can grant more charms a day.

  • A round is one calendar month, in UK time.
  • On the 1st, the final standings are saved to the Hall of Fame and every bracelet is cleared.
  • Admins choose who is in each month's round.
  • Everyone starts able to grant 3 charms a day, rising to 4, 5 and 6 as you rank up. The count resets at midnight UK time.
  • Sign in with your own six-digit passcode. Forgotten it? Ask an admin to reset it.
  • Trades are one-for-one, and an offer expires after 3 days.
